  • Kept from the code: 60% window with tabs, corner brackets on the root window only, the option toggles, travel time, the Profit/Margin switch, and Reset / Search.
  • New merges: the terminal breadcrumb sits inside the field as a suffix (as short as the tooltip version, but visible), and the travel time sits under the route arrow. The option toggles are icon-only, with the label in a tooltip (shown above the active one).

  • Dropped: the "Filter Trade Routes" panel header (the tab already says it), the 📦 emoji (it renders inconsistently in Qt on Windows), and filled Font Awesome icons.
