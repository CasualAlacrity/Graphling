What changed from today's launcher

  • Status dots become chips with a glyph and a word (✓ signed in, → sign in, ✕ stopped, ● live). Today an 8 px dot carries the state on colour alone, which breaks the grayscale rule.
  • Orange edge = "this needs you now". It's the same rule as the workbench's Source/Destination cards and the In Progress option C: one card at a time at most, and none at all while live.
  • Hotkeys render as keycaps (mono, with a 2 px bottom border) everywhere they appear: control rows, the summary line and the Voice "ready" text. A Right Shift warning sits inline, in the spirit of client-ui-capabilities.md.
  • Controls collapse while running. They're already disabled then (the code calls setEnabled(False)), so a one-line summary keeps the window short and the Voice card in view.
  • Startup as a step list instead of one changing sentence: server → overlay → speech model → listening. The download keeps its progress bar plus MB counts. A failure marks the exact step it stopped on.
  • Unchanged on purpose: the wordmark, bar, acronym, grid and glow (shared with the login pages and hero image), the 480 px width, Open logs, the status line plus version footer, and the green-then-red-on-hover STOP button.
  • The PTT sounds checkbox becomes a switch, matching the Profit/Margin switch in the workbench.
  • Placeholders: "pilot_ignis" and the version numbers are made up. The Discord avatar would replace the initial circle (marked P1 in client-ui-capabilities.md).
