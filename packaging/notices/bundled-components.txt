Native components bundled with ALICE
===================================

Python packages carry their license files in their own metadata, and the section above
was generated from those files. The components below are native libraries and model files
that come inside those packages but have no metadata of their own, so they are listed here
by hand. Full texts of the GPL and LGPL licenses are at the end of this file.

Qt 6 (via PySide6 / Shiboken6)
  License: GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0 (LGPL-3.0-only)
  Source:  https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/
           https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git/
  ALICE uses Qt and PySide6 unmodified and links to them dynamically. They ship as
  separate files in the ALICE install folder (Qt6*.dll, PySide6\ and shiboken6\). You
  may replace them with your own compatible build of the same version.

swscale; via PyAV)
  License: GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0 or later (built with --enable-version3)
  Source:  https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
